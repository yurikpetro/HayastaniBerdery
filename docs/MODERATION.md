# Moderation and content quality

## Evidence levels

- `verified` — supported by books, academic sources, or multiple independent references
- `partially-verified` — some sources exist, but details still need confirmation
- `oral` — information from local residents or guides, explicitly marked
- `needs-research` — object is known, but historical details are incomplete

## Coordinate policy

Every fortress must declare coordinate accuracy:

- `exact` — verified on site or by reliable map source
- `approximate` — correct area, but point may be offset
- `unverified` — provisional point from oral/local guidance

The product must never imply that approximate or unverified coordinates are safe navigation instructions.

## Submission workflow

1. User registers and submits a fortress proposal.
2. Proposal enters queue with status `new`.
3. Moderator reviews photos, coordinates, names, and sources.
4. Moderator may:
   - accept and publish
   - request changes
   - reject with reason
5. Accepted proposals become published fortress cards.

## Comment policy

MVP recommendation:

- post-moderation by default
- hide/delete spam or abusive comments
- allow reports on comments
- switch to pre-moderation only if spam volume grows

## Photo rights policy

Before publication, every photo should include:

- uploader identity
- author / rights holder
- confirmation that the uploader may publish the image, or that it is public domain / owned by the project

Reject photos with intrusive watermarks, unclear ownership, or obviously scraped social-media screenshots unless rights are confirmed.

## Editorial principles

- Separate facts, hypotheses, and oral testimony.
- Keep alternative names visible.
- Prefer source links over unsourced historical claims.
- Use warnings for dangerous routes, border areas, private land, and seasonal access limits.
